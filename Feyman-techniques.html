The explain-to-learn loop

Make it simple.
Make it yours.

The Feynman technique turns passive watching into proof that you understand. Use it after a lesson, then return to the room and capture the gaps as timestamped notes.

01

Choose

Pick one idea from your lesson. Keep it narrow enough to explain in two minutes.

“What exactly am I trying to understand?”
02

Explain

Write or speak as if you are teaching a curious beginner. Use plain words, not impressive words.

“How would I explain this without jargon?”
03

Find gaps

Circle every place where you hesitate, hand-wave, or reach for the source. Those are your next study targets.

“Where did my explanation become vague?”
04

Simplify

Return to the video, close the gap, and rewrite the explanation with a useful example or analogy.

“Can I make this clearer in one sentence?”

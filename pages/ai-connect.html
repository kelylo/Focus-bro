Focus Bro AI Connect.

Connect an AI assistant to your own Focus Bro workspace. You approve access in the AI provider; Focus Bro never installs a connector automatically and never asks you for an API key.

ChatGPT

Not connected

Use ChatGPT to ask about your timetable, tasks, notes, and study progress after you approve the requested permissions.

  • Open ChatGPT connector settings.
  • Paste the MCP endpoint when prompted.
  • Sign in to Focus Bro and approve access.

Claude

Not connected

Use Claude connectors to work with your Focus Bro academic workspace. Claude will ask you to sign in and approve access.

  • Open Claude connector settings.
  • Add the MCP endpoint as a connector.
  • Approve read or write permissions explicitly.
Important: The endpoint is not a secret. Your data is protected by OAuth consent and per-user permissions. Never paste passwords, Supabase service-role keys, or access tokens into chat.
